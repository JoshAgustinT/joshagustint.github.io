About

Hello again! In case you missed it, my name is Joshua. I’m from a small town south of Mexico City, close to Cholula (yes, like the hot sauce!).

I previously completed my Master’s at the University of Utah, where I was part of the MARS Research Group led by Dr. Anton Burtsev. There, I worked on in-memory hash tables that run at the speed of DRAM, and contributed to a study of how CHERI, a capability-based hardware architecture, can improve operating system kernel security. Now, as a PhD student at UBC, I’m working on running high-throughput workloads inside trusted execution environments (TEEs) without giving up performance.

I have extensive experience with C++, but I also enjoy using JavaScript to build interactive web apps (mixing in WebGL whenever I can), and I often use Python for personal scripting projects.
